Lalabella Messenger

The team chat as its own app — chat bubbles, stories, wallpapers, a home-screen widget, and the only place that sends you message notifications.

Download for Android
  1. Tap Download, then open LalabellaMessenger.apk from your notifications or Downloads.
  2. Allow Install unknown apps if Android asks.
  3. Open Lalabella Messenger and log in with your staff account.
  4. Allow Notifications. In Settings you can also turn on the floating chat head and pick a wallpaper.
Important: Settings → Apps → Lalabella Messenger → Battery → Unrestricted, so Android doesn't pause the app when the phone sleeps (Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Samsung may do this otherwise).
  • Android only for now.
  • The website and the main Lalabella app no longer notify you about chat.
