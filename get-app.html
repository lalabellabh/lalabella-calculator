Get the Lalabella App

Same system, one tap from your home screen. Notifications for messages and orders.

✅ You are already using the app.

🤖 Android Your phone

Download the app file (about 2 MB) and install it. No Play Store needed.

⬇ Download Lalabella.apk
  1. Tap the downloaded file (Lalabella.apk). Find it in Downloads if you do not see it.
  2. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser, then go back and tap Install.
  3. If you see Play Protect, choose Install anyway. It shows this because the app is not from the Play Store.
  4. Open Lalabella, log in, and tap Turn on when it asks about notifications.

Prefer no file? In Chrome, tap ⋮ → Install app (or Add to Home screen). It works the same.

🍎 iPhone / iPad Your phone

Apple does not allow app files outside the App Store, so add it to your home screen. It then opens like an app.

  1. Open this site in Safari (not Chrome or another browser).
  2. Tap the Share button ⬆︎ at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add at the top right. The Lalabella icon appears on your home screen.
  5. Open Lalabella from that icon, log in, and tap Turn on for notifications.

Notifications on iPhone need iOS 16.4 or newer, and only work when opened from the home screen icon.
