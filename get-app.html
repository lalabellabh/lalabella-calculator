Download the app

Download Messenger

  1. Tap Download, then open LalabellaMessenger.apk from your notifications or Downloads.
  2. If asked, allow installs from this browser, then Install anyway on the Play Protect warning.
  3. Open Lalabella Messenger and log in with your staff account.
  4. Allow Notifications. Chat alerts now come only from this app.

Important: Settings → Apps → Lalabella Messenger → Battery → Unrestricted, so messages keep arriving when the phone sleeps. In the app's Settings you can turn on the floating chat head, pick a wallpaper and make things transparent.

Android only for now. On iPhone, use the website chat.

Download Driver app
  1. Tap Download, then open LalabellaDriver.apk.
  2. Allow the install, then Install anyway on the Play Protect warning.
  3. Open Lalabella Driver, log in, or tap Sign up and wait for the shop to approve you.

For delivery drivers only. Set battery to Unrestricted and keep Location on while delivering.
