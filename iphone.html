Lalabella on iPhone

iPhone cannot install our Android app, so we use the Home Screen version. It looks and works the same, with alerts for new chats and orders.

Open this page in Safari to install. Other browsers on iPhone cannot add to the Home Screen with alerts.

1 · Install Lalabella (do this first)

  1. Open mysystemlla.com in Safari.
  2. Tap Share ⬆︎ at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen, keep Open as Web App on, then tap Add.
  4. Open Lalabella from the new icon (not from Safari) and log in.

2 · Turn on alerts (orders and chat)

  1. Needs iOS 16.4 or newer (Settings → General → About → iOS Version).
  2. In the Lalabella icon, tap Turn on when the bar “Get notified of new messages and orders?” shows up, then Allow.
  3. If you missed it: iPhone Settings → Notifications → Lalabella and switch Allow Notifications on.
  4. Tap a notification to open that chat or the orders page.
iPhone does not allow typing a reply inside the notification, or chat bubbles, or widgets. You tap the notification and reply in the chat (with the person's photo). This is Apple's limit for web apps.
