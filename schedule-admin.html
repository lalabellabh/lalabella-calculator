Upload a week's schedule

Upload one PDF per week. Lalabella reads the text and its position on the page to rebuild the table — review and fix anything below before saving. Saving replaces only the week you pick; other weeks are untouched, so uploading week after week builds up the full month.

Weekly schedule — everyone in one table

Pick the Sunday the week starts on. Type each person's shift per day (or Off) — leave a day blank if it isn't scheduled. Use Fill to put the same shift on a person's blank days, then change only the days that differ. Saving replaces just this week; do it week by week to build the month.

Quick option — someone with the same shift every week

For staff whose schedule doesn't change week to week (one day off, the same shift every other day), skip the PDF entirely: pick their day(s) off and shift text once, choose the month, and the whole month is generated and saved in one go — including the first/last few days that spill into the week before or after, so nothing in the calendar is left blank.
