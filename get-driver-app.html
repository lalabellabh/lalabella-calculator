Driver app

For delivery drivers. Your location is shared with the shop only while a delivery is On the way, and stops when you tap Delivered.

Scan to startOpen your phone camera and point it at the code.

Android

Download the Driver app
  1. Tap Download, then open LalabellaDriver.apk from your notifications or Downloads.
  2. If asked, allow installs from this browser. On the Play Protect warning, choose Install anyway.
  3. Open Lalabella Driver and log in with the username and password from the shop.
  4. When asked, allow Location (choose While using the app) and Notifications.
  5. Open an order and tap Start Delivery. Tap Open in Google Maps to navigate.
  6. You can lock the screen or use Google Maps. A "Tracking your delivery" notice stays on while it works.
  7. At the customer, tap Delivered (tap twice to confirm). Tracking stops.
Important: go to Settings → Apps → Lalabella Driver → Battery and choose Unrestricted (Xiaomi, Oppo, Vivo, Samsung may stop the app otherwise). Do not swipe the app away from recent apps while delivering. Keep phone Location turned on.

Not from the Play Store, so Android shows a warning. Small file, under 1 MB.

iPhone

  1. Open mysystemlla.com/driver.html in Safari.
  2. Tap Share ⬆︎, then Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open it from the new icon and log in. Allow Location when asked.
On iPhone the screen must stay on and the page open while delivering. Locking the screen or switching apps pauses the tracking (Apple's limit).

No account yet?

The shop admin creates your username and password (Delivery Map → Drivers). If you forget your password, ask the admin to reset it.
